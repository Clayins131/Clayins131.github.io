Method & limits

A small model you can audit.

Read the calculation code ↗

Cash flows, not a black box

For each payment at t years, PV = cash flow / (1 + z(t))t. Here z is an annual effective zero rate as a decimal. Coupon payments are face × coupon / frequency; principal is returned at maturity. The grid assumes full coupon periods and zero accrued interest.

Effective duration = (P− − P+) / (2PΔy). Convexity = (P− + P+ − 2P) / (PΔy²), with Δy = 0.0001. DV01 = (P− − P+) / 2, scaled to the face amount.

Interpretation comes first

Illustrative curves are not fitted to market instruments. Use bootstrapped or published zero rates with matching compounding, not policy rates or raw par yields. The euro-area curve is a generic EUR assumption; sovereign spreads are not modelled.

Scenario repricing is exact within this model. Duration/convexity is a local approximation for a parallel move only. Macro surprises, policy expectations and term premia can move in different directions; the templates are hypotheses to investigate.
